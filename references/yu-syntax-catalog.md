Grounded in Yu Guangzhong's three essays:
- **`《常态》`**: *中文的常态与变态* (Sections `01`–`08`)
- **`《西化》`**: *从西而不化到西而化之* (Part `01`, Examples `1`–`51`; Part `02` on benign Westernization)
- **`《西化论》`**: *论中文之西化* (Sections `01`–`05`, dated July 1979)

Treat these entries as contextual diagnostic lenses, not blind keyword bans. Cite to section or example number (`《常态》02`, `《西化》16–20`); never fabricate page numbers.

## Part I: Eleven Sentence-Level Diagnostics

| # | Pattern & Chinese Signal Words | Citations | Diagnostic Question | Repair Strategy & Authentic Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Weak Verbs + Abstract Nouns**<br>Signals: `作出`, `进行`, `加以`, `给予`, `予以`, `对……作出反应` | `《常态》02`<br>`《西化》16–20` | Is a generic "false limb" verb merely propping up a frozen action noun? | Delete the weak verb and activate the noun.<br>- `作出了十分热烈的反应` → `反应十分热烈`<br>- `进行了详细的研究` → `详加研究`<br>- `在老鼠的身上进行试验` → `用老鼠试验`<br>- `作出强烈的抗议` → `强烈抗议`<br>*(Keep when part of a fixed technical or legal phrase.)* |
| 2 | **Abstract Nouns as Subjects/Objects**<br>Signals: `……的提升 / 减少 / 下降 / 增长 / 推进 / 继续` + `改变 / 带动 / 影响 / 杯葛` | `《常态》02`<br>`《西化》20–25, 49–51` | Must the reader unpack a static nominalization before seeing who did what? | Put a person, concrete noun, or full event clause in the subject/object slot.<br>- `他的收入的减少改变了他的生活方式` → `他收入减少，乃改变生活方式。`<br>- `横贯公路的再度坍方，是今日的头条新闻` → `横贯公路再度坍方，是今日的头条新闻。`<br>- `杯葛这种风俗的继续` → `反对保存这种风俗` |
| 3 | **Stacked `的` & Heavy Pre-Modifiers**<br>Signals: three+ `的` in one clause; long descriptive clauses jammed before the head noun | `《常态》06`<br>`《西化》43–45` | Does a top-heavy modifier force the reader to wait too long for the core noun? | Split clauses, drop redundant `的`, or move modifiers behind the noun as open clauses.<br>- `弯弯的杨柳的稀疏的倩影` → `弯弯的杨柳投下稀疏的倩影`<br>- `夜色的来袭` → `夜色来袭`<br>- `我见到一个长得像你兄弟说话也有点像他的陌生男人` → `我见到一个陌生男人，长得像你兄弟，说话也有点像他。`<br>- `献身于革命的壮烈大业的他` → `他献身于革命的壮烈大业` |
| 4 | **Pseudo-Jargon & Suffixed Abstraction**<br>Signals: `……性`, `……型`, `……度`, `……化`, `……主义` (`可读性`, `知名度`, `热情型`, `难度很高`) | `《常态》02, 06`<br>`《西化》34–37` | Does the suffix name a real technical concept, or dress up an ordinary adjective/verb? | Strip academic camouflage in general prose; keep established domain terms (`或然率`, `百分比`, `能见度`, software `可扩展性`).<br>- `可读性颇高` → `很好看` / `引人入胜`<br>- `具有很高的知名度` → `很有名`<br>- `难度很高的技巧` → `很难的技巧`<br>- `太专业性的字眼` → `太专门的字眼`<br>- `富于爱国主义的精神` → `富于爱国精神` |
| 5 | **`之一`, `最……之一` & Hedged Qualifiers**<br>Signals: `……之一`, `最……之一`, `在一定的程度上`, `有一定的……`, `肯定` (as adverb) | `《常态》02`<br>`《西化》26–30` | Does `之一` clarify a bounded set, or hedge an obvious category? Is `一定的` actually vague? | Cut `之一` when plurality is self-evident; replace `最……之一` with `大 / 极`; make vague qualifiers specific or drop them.<br>- `作为竹林七贤之一的刘伶以嗜酒闻名` → `刘伶是竹林七贤之一，以嗜酒闻名` (or `刘伶列于竹林七贤`)<br>- `《红楼梦》是中国文学的名著之一` → `《红楼梦》是中国文学名著`<br>- `李白是中国最伟大的诗人之一` → `李白是中国极伟大的诗人`<br>- `在一定的程度上` → `酌量` / `有限度地` |

## Part II: Benign Westernization (`西而化之`) & Positive Benchmarks

Yu Guangzhong repeatedly warns against returning to a flat, impoverished vernacular (`《西化》02`, `《常态》01, 08`). When reviewing complex prose, **preserve or learn from** these benign forms:

1. **Integrated Complex Sentences (Qian Zhongshu benchmark, `《西化》02`)**:
   - *Model*: 「上帝要惩罚人类，有时来一个荒年，有时来一次瘟疫或战争，有时产生一个道德家，抱着高尚到一般人所不及的理想，更有跟他的理想成正比例的骄傲和力量。」
   - *Why it works*: Clear rhythmic pauses, active verbs driving each branch, post-positioned elaboration (`抱着……更有……`), zero weak-verb bloat (`给予我们一个荒年`, `具有着……`). Complexity itself is not a flaw.
2. **Natural Post-Positioned Phrasal Modifiers (Eileen Chang benchmark, `《西化》02`)**:
   - *Model*: 「或许他有一天还会回到她这里来，带了较优的议和条件。」
   - *Why it works*: Mirrors an English participial/prepositional tail (`bringing better terms of peace`), yet integrates cleanly after a complete Chinese main clause.
3. **Idiomatic Compression & Register Balance (`《常态》01`, `《西化》02`)**:
   - Use crisp classical/idiomatic phrases where modern prose bloats (`总而言之` over `总的来说`; `一言难尽` over `不是一句话能够说得清楚的`; `昔贤` / `详加研究`), while avoiding cliché-ridden piling up of four-character idioms.
   - Look to skilled translation benchmarks cited by Yu—Xia Ji'an (idiomatic looseness and classical tone), Qiao Zhigao (crisp conversational pacing and natural inversion), and Liang Shiqiu (blending classical terseness with vernacular).
4. **Skepticism Toward "Precision" Claims (`《西化论》04–05`)**:
   - Yu contrasts Sima Qian's 33-character *Li Guang* passage (`中石，没镞，视之，石也`) with Burton Watson's 70-word English translation, and cites Bertrand Russell and George Orwell (*Politics and the English Language*) to prove that explicit grammatical scaffolding and multi-negative hedging (`not unjustifiable assumption`) often create *illusory* precision while destroying force and rhythm. Never add `的`, pronouns, or connectors merely to imitate English explicitness.
